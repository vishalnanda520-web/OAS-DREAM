Automatic minimalist phone

A web app can’t change your phone’s settings. Android can switch to a minimal mode on a schedule by itself, so set it once and it runs every study session:

  1. Open Settings, then Digital Wellbeing, then Focus mode. Pick the apps that distract you and add a schedule for your study hours.
  2. In the same place, turn on Bedtime mode with grayscale and Do Not Disturb on a schedule for the evening.
  3. Set daily app timers for Instagram, YouTube and games.
  4. Keep only this tracker, Phone and a notes app on your home screen. Move everything else to a folder on the last page.

Menu names differ by phone brand. If you can’t find a setting, search for “Focus” in Settings.
